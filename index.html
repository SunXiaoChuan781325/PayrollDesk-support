PAYROLLDESK · iOS

使用帮助

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日

为新西兰小雇主准备的中文工资复核工具。先整理资料，再核对金额与办理进度。

界面为简体中文。当前工资规则适用于 2026 年 4 月 1 日至 2027 年 3 月 31 日的发薪日。PayrollDesk 与新西兰税务局（IRD）没有隶属关系，也没有连接 IRD 或银行。

第一次怎么用

  1. 在工作台选择“先体验 3 人虚构示例”，先熟悉流程；不要把示例记录当成真实业务。
  2. 进入“公司与备份”，设置公司资料和默认计薪周期；雇主 IRD 税号可以先留空。
  3. 在“员工资料”录入真实员工的工作方式、计薪方式和税务声明。专业词旁的“!”可以点按查看解释。
  4. 在“本期发薪”核对工资周期、实际发薪日、每日工时和适用扣款，再保存核对记录。
  5. 付款、向 IRD 申报、缴纳税款分别完成后，在“工资记录”中分别登记日期及凭据。软件记录的是你的确认，不是银行或 IRD 回执。

当前可以做什么

支持时薪或每期固定税前金额、每周／每两周／每四周／完整日历月的普通工资；支持常见 M、ME、SB、S、SH、ST、SA 及对应学生贷款税码，并按已确认的适用条件处理 PAYE、KiwiSaver、学生贷款、ESCT 和 8% 年假随薪支付。

固定工资输入的是所选周期的税前金额，不是年薪或员工净到手金额。工作类型和税码分别设置；“临时工”不等于自动符合 8% 年假随薪支付条件。

哪些需要另行处理

其他税年、不规则计税周期、未支持的税码、完整假期与假期余额、公假、奖金、入离职折算、离职结算、特殊扣款、雇主供款含薪及承包商不在当前普通工资引擎的完整处理范围。软件会对已识别的不支持情况给出提示；不要通过修改普通工时来绕过限制。

PayrollDesk 帮助复核与整理记录，不保证适合所有雇佣安排，也不代表完整工资合规审计。请核对实际合同、税务声明和适用规则。

申报与付款

本版不发送工资申报、不代缴税、不转账，也不保存 myIR 登录密码。中文教程可打开已核准的官方网站，由你在系统浏览器中继续操作。内部复核 CSV 不是 IRD 导入文件。计算完成、工资付款、工资资料申报和税款缴纳是不同步骤。

导出 PDF、CSV 和加密备份

工资核对 PDF、现金清单 PDF 和内部 CSV 通过 iOS“文件”选择器导出；它们是普通可读文件，请选择合适的保存位置。云盘位置和随后分享的收件人由你决定。

在“公司与备份”导出 .payrolliosbackup，为每份备份设置至少 12 个字符的密码。密码无法由开发者找回。恢复时先选择文件并输入密码，核对公司和记录数量，再明确确认替换；取消不会替换当前资料。恢复会在本机保留被替换的加密文件。

iOS 与 Windows 备份暂不通用。.payrollbackup 与 .payrolliosbackup 不能直接互相导入。没有设备间自动同步。

换手机、重装或出现读取错误

本地工资数据库排除在设备备份之外，设备加密密钥不会迁移到另一台设备。换手机或删除 App 前，先导出并确认你能恢复的密码加密备份。不要把普通 iCloud 设备备份当成 PayrollDesk 的工资资料备份。

若显示数据读取失败，先解锁设备并重新打开 App。错误状态下软件会停止普通保存，避免覆盖原资料。不要为了排错先删除 App；保留已有备份，联系支持并仅提供脱敏信息。

PAYROLLDESK · iOS

Support

Updated 3 October 2026

A Chinese-guided payroll review assistant for small New Zealand employers.

The app interface is Simplified Chinese. Current calculation rules cover pay dates from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. PayrollDesk is independent of Inland Revenue (IRD) and has no IRD or bank connection.

Getting started

  1. On the workspace, choose “先体验 3 人虚构示例” to explore three fictional employees. Example records do not represent real payroll.
  2. Open “公司与备份” (Company and backup) to set company details and a default pay frequency. The employer IRD number may initially be left blank.
  3. Use “员工资料” (Employees) to enter work arrangements, pay details and the employee's tax declarations. Tap the “!” beside a term for a plain-language Chinese explanation.
  4. Open “本期发薪” (Current pay run), check the pay period, payment date, daily hours and applicable deductions, then save the review record.
  5. In “工资记录” (Pay history), record wage payment, employment-information filing and tax payment separately after each has actually happened. These are your confirmations, not verified bank or IRD receipts.

Supported scope

Review ordinary hourly pay or a fixed gross amount per pay period, for weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly or complete calendar-month periods. Supported codes include M, ME, SB, S, SH, ST and SA with their student-loan variants. PAYE, KiwiSaver, student-loan deductions, ESCT and separately paid 8% holiday pay depend on the applicable settings and confirmations.

A fixed amount means gross pay for the selected period, not annual salary or take-home pay. Work arrangement and tax code are separate settings. Calling a worker casual does not establish eligibility for pay-as-you-go holiday pay.

Cases requiring separate calculation

Other tax years, irregular tax periods, unsupported tax codes, full leave and leave balances, public holidays, bonuses, partial joining/leaving periods, final pay, special deductions, salary-inclusive employer contributions and contractors are outside this ordinary-pay engine's complete scope. Known unsupported cases are flagged; do not alter ordinary hours to bypass a limitation.

PayrollDesk organises information and supports review. It is not a complete payroll compliance audit or a guarantee that every employment arrangement is covered. Check the actual agreement, employee declarations and applicable rules.

Filing and payment

The app does not submit employment information, pay taxes, transfer wages or store myIR passwords. Guidance links open approved official websites in the system browser for you to use separately. The internal review CSV is not an IRD import file. Calculating pay, paying employees, filing information and paying tax are separate steps.

Documents and encrypted backups

Payroll review PDFs, cash-payment checklist PDFs and internal CSVs export through the iOS Files picker. PDF and CSV files are readable documents; choose their destination carefully. Any cloud provider or later recipient is selected by you.

In “公司与备份”, export a .payrolliosbackup with a password of at least 12 characters. The developer cannot recover that password. To restore, choose a file, enter its password, review the company and record counts, and explicitly confirm replacement. Cancelling does not replace current data. Existing encrypted files are preserved locally during restore.

iOS and Windows backups are not interchangeable. Windows .payrollbackup files cannot be imported directly into this iOS version, or vice versa. There is no automatic device-to-device sync.

Moving devices, reinstalling or a read error

The payroll database is excluded from normal device backups, and its device-bound key does not migrate to another device. Before replacing your phone or deleting the app, export a password-encrypted backup and check that you can restore it. Do not rely on a normal iCloud device backup to restore PayrollDesk payroll data.

If a data-read error appears, unlock the device and reopen the app. Normal saves stop after a read failure to protect existing files. Do not delete the app as your first troubleshooting step. Keep your backups and contact support with redacted information.
