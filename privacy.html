PAYROLLDESK · iOS

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 3 日

PayrollDesk 的工资计算和日常记录在你的设备上处理。App 不设用户账户，也不把工资数据库发送到开发者服务器。

本政策适用于 PayrollDesk iOS App，以及由 Wenjie Lu 提供的本帮助网站和支持邮箱。

App 在本机处理哪些资料

你可以录入公司及员工名称、可选的 IRD 税号、开始工作日期、工作及计薪方式、税码、工资和扣款设置、每日工时、工资核对结果、办理日期、凭据说明及备注。这些资料用于提供你请求的工资复核和记录功能，不由开发者远程接收。

App 不含广告、跟踪器、第三方分析或开发者遥测上传服务；不读取通讯录、照片、麦克风或位置，也不要求 myIR 登录资料。App 没有 IRD、银行或云同步接口。

本机保护和设备备份

工资数据库以 AES-GCM 加密存储，设备密钥保存在 iOS 钥匙串，设置为设备解锁后可用且仅限本设备。数据文件使用 iOS 完整文件保护。工资数据目录不加入常规设备备份，密钥不通过钥匙串同步到其他设备。

保存时保留上一份有效加密文件；明确恢复时会先保留旧加密文件。进入后台时 App 显示隐私遮罩，但它不能禁止你或其他软件截屏，也不能替代设备密码。

由你决定的导出和分享

你可以通过 iOS“文件”选择器导出 PDF、CSV 或密码加密备份。PDF 和 CSV 未加密。密码加密备份为 .payrolliosbackup，与 Windows 版备份不兼容。导出到 iCloud Drive、其他文件提供商或后来分享给他人时，文件离开 App，由你选定的服务或收件人按其规则处理；这不是自动上传给开发者。

App 内用于导出的临时副本在完成或取消后清理；异常中断留下的导出暂存文件会在下次启动清理。你导出到其他位置的文件不会随 App 卸载自动删除。备份密码不会发送给我们，我们无法找回密码或远程解密备份。

官方网站、帮助网站和电子邮件

选择官方网站链接后，内容在系统浏览器打开；你在该网站的访问和输入受对应网站政策约束，PayrollDesk 不读取浏览器的登录资料。

本帮助网站仅提供静态页面，不含分析脚本、广告或数据收集表单。网站托管方 GitHub Pages 会为安全目的记录访客 IP 地址；详情见 GitHub 隐私声明。

如果你主动发送支持邮件，我们会收到你的邮件地址及你选择提供的内容，用于回复、排查问题和处理相关请求。请只发必要的脱敏信息。支持通信会保留至处理请求及适用义务所需的时间；你可以联系下方邮箱提出查询或删除支持邮件的请求。App 的本机工资数据库不在我们可访问或删除的范围内。

保留和删除

工资资料留在 App 本机数据区，直到被你明确恢复的备份替换或删除 App。恢复时保留的旧加密副本也属于本机资料。需要清除所有本机工资资料时，使用 iOS 的“删除 App”；“卸载 App”／Offload App 会保留文稿与数据，不能当作删除。

删除 App 后，系统钥匙串可能继续保留一项加密密钥；它本身不包含员工资料或工资记录。删除 App 不会删除你已导出的文件、收件人的副本或文件服务内的备份。删除前请另行保留你依法需要留存的记录。

政策更新与联系方式

如果后续版本增加联网服务或改变数据处理方式，我们会更新政策和适用的 App 隐私说明。本版没有这些联网功能。

Privacy policy

Updated 3 October 2026

PayrollDesk performs payroll calculations and keeps everyday records on your device. The app has no user account and does not send its payroll database to a developer server.

This policy covers the PayrollDesk iOS app, this help website and support email provided by Wenjie Lu.

Information processed on your device

You can enter company and employee names, optional IRD numbers, start dates, work and pay arrangements, tax codes, pay and deduction settings, daily hours, payroll review results, completion dates, reference notes and other notes. This information enables the review and record-keeping functions you request; it is not remotely received by the developer.

The app includes no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics or developer telemetry upload service. It does not access contacts, photos, microphone or location, or request myIR credentials. It has no IRD, bank or cloud-sync interface.

Local protection and device backups

The payroll database is encrypted with AES-GCM. Its device key is held in the iOS Keychain, accessible when the device is unlocked and restricted to this device. Data files use complete iOS file protection. The payroll data directory is excluded from normal device backup, and its key does not sync through iCloud Keychain.

Saving preserves the previous valid encrypted file; explicit restore preserves old encrypted files before replacement. The app shows a privacy cover while inactive. This does not prevent screenshots or replace a device passcode.

Exports and sharing you choose

You can export PDFs, CSVs and password-encrypted backups using the iOS Files picker. PDF and CSV exports are not encrypted. Encrypted backups use .payrolliosbackup, which is not interchangeable with the Windows backup format. When you export to iCloud Drive or another file provider, or later share a file, your selected service or recipient handles it under its own terms. This is not an automatic upload to the developer.

Temporary export staging copies are cleaned after completion or cancellation; interrupted export staging is cleaned at the next app launch. Files exported elsewhere are not deleted when you delete the app. Backup passwords are not sent to us, and we cannot recover them or remotely decrypt your backups.

Official links, this website and email

Official links open in the system browser. Your visits and information entered there are subject to the destination website's policy; PayrollDesk does not read the browser's login information.

This help website consists of static pages with no analytics scripts, advertisements or data-collection forms. Its hosting provider, GitHub Pages, logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you voluntarily email support, we receive your email address and the content you choose to send so we can respond, troubleshoot and handle your request. Send only necessary, redacted information. Support correspondence is kept for as long as needed for the request and applicable obligations. You may contact the address below about access to or deletion of support correspondence. We cannot access or remotely delete the app's local payroll database.

Retention and deletion

Payroll records remain in the app's local data area until explicitly replaced by a restored backup or the app is deleted. Preserved encrypted recovery copies are also local records. To remove all app-local payroll data, use iOS Delete App; Offload App retains documents and data and is not equivalent to deletion.

iOS Keychain may retain an encryption-key item after app deletion. The key itself contains no employee details or payroll records. Deleting the app does not delete your exported files, recipients' copies or backups held by your selected file service. Retain any records you are required to keep before deleting data.

Policy changes and contact

If a later version adds network services or changes data handling, we will update this policy and the relevant App Privacy details. Those network features are not part of this version.
